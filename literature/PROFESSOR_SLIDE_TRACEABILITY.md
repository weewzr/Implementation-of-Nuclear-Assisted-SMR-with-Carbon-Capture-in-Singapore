## Immediate scientific consequences

1. The professor's strongest transferable contribution is **methodological**: safety requires an accident sequence, frequency/consequence assessment, barriers, source term, release, dose and criterion—not the statement “the reactor is safe.”
2. The recovered ~400 m discussion is **not a Singapore EPZ**. It is a prompt to investigate the technical basis of advanced-reactor EPZ sizing.
3. HTR-PM literature provides a design-specific example of an EPZ on the order of several hundred metres, but that result is not transferable to this GTHTR300C-class screening project without a project accident spectrum, mechanistic source term, site meteorology and dose analysis.
4. Waste, cooling, floating/offshore, underground siting and research-reactor comments are research questions until reactor/site-specific evidence is established.
5. Slides containing cost or maturity expectations are not used as quantitative evidence without underlying sources.
