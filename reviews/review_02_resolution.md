## R2-B02 — Integrated temperature-resolved candidate energy balance

**Final disposition: OPEN BLOCKER.**

### Original scientific issue
The earlier ~162 MWth nuclear-service value was a bounded midpoint assembled
from conventional furnace-service and capture/recycle corrections. Review 2
required a closed, temperature-resolved candidate heat cascade with no
double-counting, and nuclear duty obtained as the residual of that physical
balance rather than inserted as a representative point.

### Current scientific state
Substantial prerequisite work is complete:
- conventional IEAGHG radiant duty and syngas-WHB recovery are
  temperature-resolved and source-validated;
- NIST/IAPWS properties cover the relevant temperature ranges;
- recycle sensible heating and compression are represented;
- secondary-helium duty/temperature-approach/IHX/pressure-drop constraints are
  implemented;
- the canonical recycle composition is now generated by the thermodynamic
  full-species solver.

However, the candidate energy model has **not yet been rebuilt around that
canonical thermodynamic recycle state**. Existing functions such as
`converged_recycle_reaction_heat_delta_mw` and
`converged_htgr_service_source_bounded_mw` belong to the retired reduced
0.737 recycle surrogate. The repository therefore does not yet contain one
closed candidate control volume that reconciles reaction duty, feed/recycle
sensible heating, steam generation/recovery, capture regeneration, heat
recovery and nuclear heat residual on the same solved state.

### Code/equation location
Relevant completed foundations are in `model/src/lib.rs`:
- `major_stream_enthalpy_mw`;
- `reconstructed_reformer_whb_duty_mw`;
- `independent_radiant_duty_envelope_mw`;
- `nuclear_heat_integration_check`;
- thermodynamic full-species recycle functions listed under R2-B01.

The missing object is a canonical candidate energy-ledger function consuming
the thermodynamic recycle solution.

### Validation/CI evidence
The conventional energy and heat-integration component tests pass, but no CI
test currently demonstrates closure of the **new full candidate energy
balance** or reproduces nuclear duty as its residual.

### Acceptance-criterion status
- temperature-resolved conventional reference: **PASS**
- candidate heat cascade based on current full-species recycle: **FAIL / NOT IMPLEMENTED**
- no double counting across reaction/sensible/steam/capture/recovery duties:
  **NOT YET DEMONSTRATED**

R2-B02 remains a genuine blocker. This is the highest-priority unresolved
Review-2 scientific task.
