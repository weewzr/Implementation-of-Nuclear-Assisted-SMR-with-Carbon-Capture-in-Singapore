# Attachments

This directory is the intake area for user-supplied material that may support the manuscript but is not automatically canonical scientific evidence.

Place items here such as:

- CN4252 mid-term reflection and other module-specific reflections;
- externally generated Figures 1–3 or replacement equipment/reactor artwork;
- reference images and visual-layout examples;
- instructor/module material supplied by the user;
- other images proposed for inclusion in the report.

## Important use rule

Uploading a file here does **not** automatically make its scientific claims, numbers, or artwork canonical.

Before material is used in the report:

1. identify what the attachment is and where it came from;
2. distinguish user/module material from external literature;
3. check any scientific numbers against the canonical provenance/model;
4. record licensing/attribution where relevant;
5. if replacing a generated manuscript figure, preserve the physical topology, labels, numerical meaning, and caption qualifications defined in `docs/FIGURE_1_2_3_SPECIFICATION.md`;
6. update the LaTeX manuscript deliberately rather than silently substituting an attachment.

The canonical manuscript remains `paper/main.tex` and its active section files. Canonical numerical provenance remains under `results/final_design/`.
