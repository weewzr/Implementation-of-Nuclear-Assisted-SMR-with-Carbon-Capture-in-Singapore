### REF-SUP-001  Nexant and Bechtel 2020, DICE-CRCC

- Title: 100 MWe Coal-Fired Direct Injection Carbon Engine (DICE) Compound Reheat Combined Cycle (CRCC) with 90 Percent Post-Combustion CO2 Capture: Final Report for Pre-FEED Study
- Authors and year: Nexant, Inc.; Bechtel Power and Infrastructure (2020)
- Source: U.S. Department of Energy contract 89243319CFE000025, 30 April 2020, 481 pp.
- DOI or official URL: https://netl.doe.gov/sites/default/files/2020-05/DICE-CRCC-Pre-FEED-Final-Report_1.pdf
- Filename: `Nexant_Bechtel_2020_MEA_Capture_Plant_Reference_Design.pdf` (in this folder)
- Primary engineering purpose: Reference_Design
- Secondary engineering uses: Literature_Values
- Equipment supported: MEA absorber; stripper; regenerator reboiler; rich/lean amine exchanger; lean amine pump and cooler; condenser (plant-scale equipment)
- Equations: Partial. Literature parameter values: Yes. Validation data: No.
- Important pages, tables and equations: Vol. II Design Basis Report (printed pp. 53-73); Vol. III Technology Gap Analysis (pp. 74-128); Vol. IV Performance Results (pp. 129-237); Vol. V Cost Results (pp. 238-268); reboiler and condenser in the PCC list (PDF p. 17); rich/lean amine exchanger (PDF p. 185); lean amine pump (PDF p. 197); lean amine cooler in the equipment list (PDF p. 215)
- Missing supplementary materials: None identified
- Priority: SUPPORTING
- Notes: 30 wt% MEA unit capturing 90% of the CO2 in coal flue gas, not syngas. Combined Volumes I-V plus appendices.

### REF-SUP-002  IEAGHG 2022, 2022-07

- Title: Low-Carbon Hydrogen from Natural Gas: Global Roadmap
- Authors and year: IEAGHG (IEA Greenhouse Gas R&D Programme); work undertaken by Element Energy and CE Delft (2022)
- Source: IEAGHG Technical Report 2022-07, August 2022, 144 pp. in the file
- DOI or official URL: IEAGHG Technical Report 2022-07; official URL not verified
- Filename: `IEAGHG_2022_SMR_CCS_Reference_Design_Literature_Values.pdf` (in this folder)
- Primary engineering purpose: Reference_Design
- Secondary engineering uses: Literature_Values
- Equipment supported: Conventional SMR with CCS; WGS reactor (inlet conditions); syngas cooler (one-line mention); MEA absorber (post-combustion capture on SMR flue gas)
- Equations: Partial. Literature parameter values: Yes. Validation data: No.
- Important pages, tables and equations: SMR with CCS process description and Figure 34 (PDF p. 58); MEA post-combustion capture on the reformer flue gas and Figure 35 capture locations (PDF p. 59); syngas composition before the WGS reactor (PDF pp. 18 and 32); capture solvents (PDF p. 44); Tables 19 and 20, SMR process data and mass and energy balance (PDF p. 114). PDF page = printed page + 25
- Missing supplementary materials: None identified
- Priority: SUPPORTING
- Notes: Complete (144 pp). A techno-economic roadmap for blue hydrogen, not an equipment design. The SMR with CCS case captures 90% of the CO2 from the reformer flue gas with MEA, not from syngas. The product gas is cooled to about 340 to 350 C before the WGS reactor (PDF p. 58). The WGS reaction enthalpy printed on PDF p. 58 (206 kJ/mol) repeats the reforming value and contradicts the text on the same page that calls the shift slightly exothermic, so it should not be used.
