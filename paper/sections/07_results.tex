\section{Results}
The central Gate-5 experiment evaluates 64 physically paired nuclear-assisted cases spanning reformer temperature and pressure, PSA recovery, capture fraction, upstream/auxiliary-carbon conditions and heat/fixed-cost conditions. No case satisfies both CN4252 thresholds simultaneously:
\begin{equation}
N_{\mathrm{joint\ pass}}=0\quad\text{of}\quad N=64.
\label{eq:joint-pass}
\end{equation}
The conservative credible case produces non-positive lifecycle abatement and therefore fails both the annual-abatement and economic criteria.

Figure~\ref{fig:threshold-map} is generated directly from the canonical Rust-produced Gate-5 CSV during every paper build. The dashed lines are the assignment thresholds, so the upper-left region is the joint-pass region: annual avoided emissions greater than \(0.25\) MtCO2e/y and forward abatement cost below S\$100/tCO2e. Infinite-cost cases, which arise when lifecycle abatement is non-positive, are retained in the canonical CSV but are not assigned an artificial finite plotting coordinate.
