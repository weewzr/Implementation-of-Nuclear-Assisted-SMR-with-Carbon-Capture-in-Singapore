\section{Comparator analysis}
IEAGHG Case 1A~\cite{ieaghg2017smr} provides the strongest source-backed conventional SMR+CCS comparator on the common H2 scale. Using source direct-emission values, its annual direct avoided CO2 exceeds 0.25 Mt/y. Thus the assignment-scale abatement requirement is not unique to nuclear integration.

The source Case-1A capture-avoidance cost is retained in its original EUR2014 basis. Singapore T\&S scenario contributions are reported separately in SGD per tonne avoided after correcting the captured/avoided denominator. These quantities are not added across currency years and called a Singapore project cost. Current evidence therefore does not establish whether Case 1A passes the S\$100/t threshold in Singapore, nor does it establish a definitive eSMR/electrolysis winner.


Table~\ref{tab:case1a-comparator} exposes the evidence classes rather than collapsing unlike quantities into a single comparison. The first five rows are direct IEAGHG source values~\cite{ieaghg2017smr}. Annual avoided CO$_2$ and the non-T\&S CAC are reconstructed from those source values. The Singapore T\&S range is retained only as the project's screening scenario input, and its contribution is converted from a captured-CO$_2$ denominator to an avoided-CO$_2$ denominator. No EUR2014-to-SGD escalation or foreign-exchange conversion is introduced without an explicit evidence basis.
