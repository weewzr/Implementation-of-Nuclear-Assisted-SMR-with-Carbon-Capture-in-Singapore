\section{Comparator context}
IEAGHG Case 1A conventional SMR+CCS remains the principal external comparator because it provides a transparent merchant-hydrogen capture basis~\cite{ieaghg2017smr}. On the common production-hours basis it exceeds the 0.25 Mt/y direct-abatement scale. Its fully matched Singapore forward S\$/t remains unresolved because the source non-T\&S economics and Singapore cross-border T\&S conditions do not yet form a validated common project basis.

Electrified SMR and electrolysis remain relevant alternatives, but this project does not contain sufficiently matched Singapore lifecycle and forward-economic evidence to rank them against the final nuclear-assisted design. The final result is therefore an assessment of whether the selected nuclear-assisted configuration can satisfy the CN4252 thresholds under its declared assumptions, not a declaration that it is Singapore's universally preferred hydrogen pathway.
