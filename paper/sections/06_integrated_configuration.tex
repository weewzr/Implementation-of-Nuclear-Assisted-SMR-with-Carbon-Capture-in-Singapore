\section{Final integrated configuration}
Figure~\ref{fig:final-system} is the operative final architecture. Natural gas and steam enter a single high-temperature reformer. Nuclear heat is delivered through the HTGR/IHX/secondary-helium chain; downstream WGS, CO$_2$ separation and PSA produce H$_2$, captured CO$_2$ and a residual carbon-bearing stream. The reactor's remaining thermal output supports cogenerated electricity.

The heat-duty match is physically important. INL Case 6 requires 176.8 MWth, below the 600 MWth reactor rating but slightly above the 170 MWth reference GTHTR300C IHX. The final cost basis therefore uses the source doubled-IHX/secondary-loop sensitivity. This avoids assigning either insufficient heat-transfer hardware or free unused reactor capacity.
