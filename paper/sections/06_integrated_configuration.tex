\section{Integrated nuclear-assisted SMR+CCS configuration}
The corrected configuration begins from the IEAGHG NG/steam-derived external feed. C2+ species are reduced through an element-conserving pre-reforming screen before coupled SMR/WGS equilibrium. Shifted-gas CO2 is removed before PSA; purge-derived CO/CH4 is oxidised and captured separately.

The high-grade heat cascade enforces finite terminal approaches. Candidate MDEA regeneration scales with solved CO2 throughput, while CO2 and tail compression remain stream-specific. Secondary-helium mass flow follows the corrected candidate duty; pressure loss and circulator electricity are bounded engineering quantities rather than detailed piping predictions. The high-temperature interface is benchmarked against the JAERI GTHTR300C cogeneration concept~\cite{jaeri2004gthtr300c}; that source is used as an engineering precedent, not as a selected commercial reactor for Singapore.


Figure~\ref{fig:integrated-system} redraws the model topology as an original project-specific schematic. It is not a vendor process-flow diagram and does not imply detailed equipment sizing. Its purpose is to make the computational control volumes and energy interfaces explicit: external natural-gas/steam feed, reforming and shift, high-pressure process-carbon capture, PSA hydrogen separation, inert-controlled recycle/purge, purge-carbon oxidation/capture, and the nuclear primary-to-secondary helium heat interface.
