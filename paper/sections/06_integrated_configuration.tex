\section{Final integrated configuration}
Figure~\ref{fig:final-system} shows the operative process architecture. Natural gas and steam enter the reformer; high-temperature heat reaches it through an intermediate heat exchanger (IHX) and isolated secondary-helium loop. Downstream water-gas shift (WGS), CO$_2$ capture and pressure-swing adsorption (PSA) produce hydrogen. The nuclear primary helium does not contact the process gas.

Thermally, the design therefore makes a limited but defensible claim: the selected 600 MWth reactor architecture has a source-supported 370 MWth heat branch that exceeds the 176.8 MWth process requirement, while the INL 900/466$^\circ$C helium state supports the required heat transfer. No exact project gross or net export MWe is claimed.
