\section{Final integrated configuration}
Figure~\ref{fig:final-system} shows the operative screening architecture. Natural gas and steam enter the Steam Methane Reformer (SMR); downstream Water-Gas Shift (WGS), amine-based carbon-dioxide capture and Pressure Swing Adsorption (PSA) produce hydrogen. Nuclear heat reaches the reformer through an Intermediate Heat Exchanger (IHX) and a physically separate secondary-helium loop. The reactor primary helium therefore transfers heat across the IHX but does not enter the chemical plant.

Figure~\ref{fig:proposed-orientation} provides the submission-facing integrated architecture; it is introduced earlier so the physical concept is visible before the detailed derivations.



Thermally, the design makes a limited but traceable claim: the selected 600 MWth reactor architecture has a source-supported approximately 370 MWth heat branch that exceeds the 176.8 MWth process requirement, while the INL 900/466$^\circ$C secondary-helium state supports the source heat-transfer balance. The figure deliberately distinguishes the approximately 170 MWth reference physical IHX from the approximately 370 MWth source branch. No exact project gross or net export MWe is claimed. PSA tail-gas/purge/recycle details remain governed by the canonical process boundary rather than a visually simplified full-recycle arrow.
