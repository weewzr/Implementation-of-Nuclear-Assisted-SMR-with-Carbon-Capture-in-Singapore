\section{Model verification and validation}
Verification proceeds at equation, unit-operation and integrated-system levels. Reaction operators conserve C/H/O/N; the canonical external plant boundary closes normalized C/H/O/N and total mass residuals below $10^{-6}$. Captured CO2 is explicitly absent from the downstream PSA/recycle state. Equilibrium solvers reject unbracketed roots and invalid property temperatures.

The conventional reference is independently checked against IEAGHG material and energy data. Temperature-resolved reconstruction of the reformer radiant section agrees with the source approximately 96 MW benchmark to within 5\% at the nearest admissible source-limited bound. The exact primary-reformer inlet is unpublished, so this is explicitly a source-limited validation rather than a unique-state reconstruction.

Integrated adversarial tests reject zero process-temperature approach, require identical CCS/energy MDEA duties, preserve auxiliary electricity in lifecycle accounting, and preserve adverse threshold outcomes. Passing CI is treated as reproducibility evidence, not by itself as scientific validation.
