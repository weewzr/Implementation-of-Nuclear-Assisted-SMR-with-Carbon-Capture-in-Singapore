\section{Discussion}
The final design deliberately separates three evidence layers. INL TEV-953/961 supplies the chemical-process design and the 176.8 MWth high-temperature duty. Nishihara et al. supplies a coherent mature GTHTR300C hardware/economic architecture with a 370 MWth source hydrogen-heat branch and 88 MWe from the complementary 230 MWth power branch. The project then maps the smaller INL heat draw onto that architecture using the source-implied power conversion; this mapping is derived rather than directly published.

This correction matters because earlier project work conflated the 170 MWth/202 MWe GTHTR300C variant with Nishihara's 370 MWth/88 MWe economic variant. The final calculation no longer does so. The 176.8 MWth INL duty is below the 370 MWth source heat branch, and the doubled-IHX/secondary-loop case is retained only as an adverse cost sensitivity rather than a capacity argument.

The lifecycle abatement result remains dominated by the large same-output direct-emissions difference and lower natural-gas use. Economically, the zero-electricity-value case is retained as the conservative test so the threshold result does not require a lucrative offtake. Electricity values of S$100--200/MWh are sensitivities. Cross-border CCS availability, mature-design reactor economics and the project-derived part-load cogeneration mapping remain important limitations.
