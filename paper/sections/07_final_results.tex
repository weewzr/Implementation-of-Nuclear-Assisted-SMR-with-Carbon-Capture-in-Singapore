The zero-value case is the principal cost-robustness screen: the reactor heat-plus-power economic burden remains charged but exported electricity receives no value. The corrected model remains below S\$100/tCO$_2$e in that case. S\$100, S\$150 and S\$200/MWh cases are market-value sensitivities, not guaranteed offtake. The negative central screening cost means only that annual candidate net cost is below the common baseline under the stated assumptions.

The Nishihara doubled-IHX/secondary-loop \emph{cost} case is retained as an adverse sensitivity; it is not evidence of increased exchanger capacity.

\subsection{CN4252 interpretation}
The corrected calculation passes both numerical thresholds under the declared model assumptions, but the independent verification remains open until the reviewer accepts the INL-to-JAEA part-load mapping and corrected economics. The result therefore remains a \textbf{CONDITIONAL MODEL RESULT}, not evidence of an existing Singapore commercial project.
