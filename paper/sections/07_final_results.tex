The zero-electricity-value case is the controlling economic test. It charges the full selected Nishihara source-product economic burden, CCS, integration, T\&S and candidate natural gas, subtracts the common baseline natural-gas cost, and gives no revenue for cogenerated electricity. This deliberately prevents an uncertain power-cycle mapping from controlling the CN4252 conclusion.

\subsection{CN4252 interpretation}
The corrected calculation passes the annual-abatement threshold by approximately 667 ktCO$_2$e/y. Its controlling zero-value cost remains below S\$100/tCO$_2$e under the declared mature source-cost allocation. The result remains a \textbf{CONDITIONAL MODEL RESULT pending independent reviewer re-verification}, not evidence of an existing Singapore commercial project.
