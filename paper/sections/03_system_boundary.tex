\section{System boundary and comparison basis}
The canonical candidate boundary is
\[
\text{NG+steam}\rightarrow\text{pre-reforming}\rightarrow\text{SMR/WGS}
\rightarrow\text{CO2 removal}\rightarrow\text{PSA}\rightarrow
\text{purge/recycle}.
\]
The nuclear boundary supplies high-grade reformer heat and residual low-grade capture duty through a secondary-helium loop. The lifecycle boundary adds upstream natural gas, nuclear lifecycle allocation, auxiliary electricity and CO2 transport/storage.

Captured CO2 is removed physically before downstream PSA/recycle. PSA tail gas is split between recycle and an inert-control purge; purge carbon is oxidised and routed to the capture chain. The common H2 basis is fixed across comparisons. Conventional IEAGHG Case 1A is retained as the strongest source-backed CCS comparator. Electrified SMR and electrolysis are discussed only to the extent supported by matched evidence; unmatched cost bases are not used to rank technologies.
