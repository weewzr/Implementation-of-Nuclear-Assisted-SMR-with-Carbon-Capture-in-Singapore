\section{Design basis and system boundary}\label{sec:design-basis}
Before calculating performance, the study fixes what is being compared and classifies the principal inputs. The candidate follows the Idaho National Laboratory (INL) Case-6 process:
\[
\text{natural gas + steam}\rightarrow\text{SMR}\rightarrow\text{WGS}
\rightarrow\text{CO$_2$ capture}\rightarrow\text{PSA}\rightarrow\text{H$_2$}.
\]
The reference is INL's conventional no-capture SMR at the same 130 million standard cubic feet per day (MMSCFD) hydrogen service~\cite{inltev953}. Using a common product output prevents the emissions comparison from being driven by plant size.

\subsection{Process and heat design basis}
The main \textbf{literature inputs} are the INL Case-6 process states: 871$^\circ$C reformer outlet, steam-to-carbon ratio 3.0, 88\% PSA hydrogen recovery, 925$^\circ$C reactor outlet, 900$^\circ$C secondary-helium supply, approximately 466$^\circ$C secondary-helium return, 78.49 kg/s source helium flow and 176.8 MWth reformer heat duty~\cite{inltev953,inltev961}. The source process produces 130 MMSCFD H$_2$ and uses 34.0 MMSCFD natural gas~\cite{inltev961}.

The reactor \textbf{design basis} is a 600 MWth GTHTR300C-class architecture from JAEA/Nishihara literature~\cite{nishihara2007potential}. The project combines that hardware/scale precedent with the INL 925/900/871$^\circ$C operating point; it is therefore GTHTR300C-informed rather than an exact reproduction of a published GTHTR300C plant.

\subsection{Annualisation and lifecycle boundary}
Availability is a \textbf{source-anchored design assumption}: 85\%, giving 7,446 operating hours per year~\cite{nishihara2007potential}. The lifecycle boundary includes direct process emissions, upstream natural-gas supply, a declared nuclear-heat lifecycle allocation, incremental process electricity and CO$_2$ transport/storage. The upstream-gas and nuclear factors are global proxies rather than Singapore-specific measurements; the transport/storage lifecycle factor is a project screening assumption.

\subsection{Economic boundary}
The economic comparison includes baseline/candidate natural gas, the full selected reactor source-product economic burden, capture capital annualisation, a declared integration/site allowance and CO$_2$ Transport and Storage (T\&S). Project electricity revenue is set to \textbf{S\$0/y} because the model does not contain a validated off-design project power-output calculation. This is a conservative project boundary, not a market-price forecast.

The detailed source/assumption/derived classification is preserved in the numerical-provenance register. In the sections that follow, literature values are cited where introduced, assumptions are named explicitly, and quantities such as annual hydrogen, avoided emissions and remaining reactor capacity are reported as \textbf{derived results}, not as literature measurements.
