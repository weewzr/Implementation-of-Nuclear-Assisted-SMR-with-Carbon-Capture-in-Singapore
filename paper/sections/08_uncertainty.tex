\section{Sensitivity, uncertainty and falsification}

\subsection{Canonical 64-case domain and forward-cost inputs}
The coupled design is the Cartesian product of the six binary dimensions in Table~\ref{tab:domain}. The carbon/energy-price and heat/fixed-cost dimensions are deliberately paired corners, not four independently crossed variables; hence the design contains exactly $2^6=64$ cases. Values labelled as project scenarios are not Singapore forecasts.

For each design point, baseline annual cost is unabated conventional-SMR natural-gas energy at the same 8322 h/y basis. Candidate annual cost is fresh-feed natural gas plus high-side nuclear process heat plus CCS/tail-compression/helium-circulator electricity plus the paired bundled fixed annual allowance. Thus
\begin{equation}
C_{\rm cand}=C_{\rm NG,fresh}+C_{\rm nuclear\ heat}+C_{\rm aux\ electricity}+C_{\rm fixed},
\end{equation}
followed by Eq.~\eqref{eq:abatement-cost}. The Gate-5 grid does not add a separate CCS transport/storage cash charge: the S\$80--120 million/y term is an explicit bundled project screening allowance. Natural-gas prices S\$15--20/GJ and the fixed-cost range are author-declared scenario assumptions; electricity S\$150--200/MWh is a screening range consistent with 2025 Singapore wholesale prices~\cite{emaenergyprices}; the S\$5.69/GJ lower nuclear-heat anchor is a project conversion of JAEA's 0.7 JPY/MJ HTGR heat-cost assumption~\cite{jaea2014economics}, while S\$8/GJ is an author-declared adverse sensitivity.

The 64-case design maps joint threshold behaviour rather than varying parameters until a favourable point appears. Cases are partitioned into abatement-only failure, cost-only failure, both-threshold failure and joint pass. Local driver contrasts quantify the effect of reformer temperature, reformer pressure, PSA recovery, capture fraction, carbon/electricity conditions and heat/fixed-cost assumptions on both annual abatement and S\$/t.

Table~\ref{tab:binding-counts} is populated directly from the generated binding-count CSV. It is a compact statement of the threshold-failure topology and provides an independent manuscript-level reconciliation to the 64-case design and Eq.~\eqref{eq:joint-pass}.

The uncertainty study therefore serves two purposes: it identifies binding constraints and actively attempts to falsify the nuclear hypothesis. The absence of a joint passing point in the declared design is retained as a scientific finding rather than treated as a numerical failure.

Figure~\ref{fig:driver-effects} reports the six local one-factor driver contrasts relative to the same favourable anchor used by Gate-5 Experiment 02. The effects are normalized by the corresponding CN4252 threshold: \(\Delta A/(250{,}000~\mathrm{tCO_2e/y})\) for annual abatement and \(\Delta C/(100~\mathrm{S\$/tCO_2e})\) for cost. These quantities are sensitivity contrasts, not global causal coefficients; coupled behaviour remains represented by the 64-case design. The \texttt{gas\_aux\_carbon\_price} perturbation produces non-positive lifecycle abatement, so its abatement cost is infinite and has no finite cost-bar ordinate. That falsification state remains explicit in the canonical driver CSV and is omitted only from the finite cost plotting layer.
