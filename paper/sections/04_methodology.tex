\section{Governing equations and methodology}
\subsection{Reaction equilibrium}
The screening reformer uses ideal-gas equilibrium for steam methane reforming,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{CH_4+H_2O\rightleftharpoons CO+3H_2},
\end{equation}
and WGS,
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{CO+H_2O\rightleftharpoons CO_2+H_2}.
\end{equation}
At fixed $T,P$, reaction extents are solved from
\begin{equation}
\ln\left(\frac{Q_r}{K_r(T)}\right)=0,
\end{equation}
subject to non-negative species inventories. Temperature-dependent standard-state properties use interval-safe NIST Shomate correlations~\cite{nistwebbook}.

\subsection{Recycle and purge}
For nonzero external nitrogen feed, steady state requires
\begin{equation}
\dot n_{N_2,\mathrm{purge}}=\dot n_{N_2,\mathrm{fresh}},
\end{equation}
with the full H2/H2O/CO/CO2/CH4/N2 recycle composition converged internally. An outer bracketed solve imposes the fixed H2 product target.

\subsection{Energy cascade}
Total stream enthalpy gives the reformer external duty without separately adding reaction and sensible terms:
\begin{equation}
Q_{\mathrm{ref}}=\dot H_{\mathrm{out}}(T_{\mathrm{ref}})
-\dot H_{\mathrm{in}}(T_{\mathrm{in}}).
\end{equation}
Recoverable WHB heat is credited at most once against candidate-scaled MDEA regeneration duty. The reference heat hierarchy uses a 900~$^\circ$C process hot end, 920~$^\circ$C secondary-helium hot end and 950~$^\circ$C primary outlet. The 950~$^\circ$C reactor-outlet and 170~MW IHX engineering precedent is source-anchored to the JAERI GTHTR300C concept~\cite{jaeri2004gthtr300c}; the finite 20~K process-side and 30~K IHX approaches are declared screening assumptions, not source values.

\subsection{Lifecycle and economics}
Annual avoided emissions are
\begin{equation}
E_{\mathrm{avoided}}=(I_{\mathrm{base}}-I_{\mathrm{cand}})
\dot m_{H_2}t_{\mathrm{op}},
\end{equation}
and forward abatement cost is
\begin{equation}
C_{\mathrm{abatement}}=
\frac{C_{\mathrm{cand}}-C_{\mathrm{base}}}
{E_{\mathrm{avoided}}}.
\end{equation}
S\$100/tCO2e is used only as an evaluation threshold, never as an input to the forward cost calculation.
