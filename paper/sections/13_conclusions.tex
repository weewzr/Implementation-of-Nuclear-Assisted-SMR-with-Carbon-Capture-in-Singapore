\section{Conclusions}
A reproducible, source-anchored screening model was developed to test nuclear-assisted SMR+CCS against the CN4252 Singapore criteria. The corrected model closes plant-wide mass and elemental balances, enforces explicit CO2 removal and inert purge, couples equilibrium reforming/WGS with a finite-approach nuclear heat cascade, propagates CCS and auxiliary loads into lifecycle emissions, and calculates abatement cost forward.

The principal result is unambiguous for the tested domain: none of 64 coupled nuclear-assisted cases satisfies both greater than 0.25 MtCO2e/y and less than S\$100/tCO2e. The conservative credible case fails both. Conventional IEAGHG Case 1A exceeds the annual direct-abatement scale on the same H2 production basis, but available evidence does not establish its Singapore S\$/t outcome on a common currency/year basis.

Accordingly, this study does not support claiming nuclear-assisted SMR+CCS as the preferred Singapore CN4252 solution. Nor does it establish a replacement compliant winner: Case 1A clears the direct annual-abatement scale but lacks matched Singapore cost evidence, while electrified SMR and electrolysis lack sufficiently matched lifecycle/economic evidence in this project for a definitive ranking. The original fixed-scale conclusion remains: simultaneous threshold compliance is not demonstrated in the 64-case Gate-5 domain. The Review-5-remediated deployment-scale extension does not preserve the earlier conditional pass: at the directly supported JAEA helium-heated reforming state, lifecycle abatement is non-positive and no finite deployment scale can satisfy the annual threshold. GTHTR300C cogeneration economics and Singapore electricity-value sensitivities therefore remain diagnostic rather than compliance evidence.
