\section{Conclusions}
A reproducible, source-anchored screening model was developed to test nuclear-assisted SMR+CCS against the CN4252 Singapore criteria. The corrected model closes plant-wide mass and elemental balances, enforces explicit CO2 removal and inert purge, couples equilibrium reforming/WGS with a finite-approach nuclear heat cascade, propagates CCS and auxiliary loads into lifecycle emissions, and calculates abatement cost forward.

The principal result is unambiguous for the tested domain: none of 64 coupled nuclear-assisted cases satisfies both greater than 0.25 MtCO2e/y and less than S\$100/tCO2e. The conservative credible case fails both. Conventional IEAGHG Case 1A exceeds the annual direct-abatement scale on the same H2 production basis, but available evidence does not establish its Singapore S\$/t outcome on a common currency/year basis.

Accordingly, this study does not support claiming nuclear-assisted SMR+CCS as the preferred Singapore CN4252 solution. It supports a narrower conclusion: the concept is technically screenable and scientifically testable, but simultaneous threshold compliance is not demonstrated within the evidence-backed domain, and comparative economic evidence remains incomplete.
