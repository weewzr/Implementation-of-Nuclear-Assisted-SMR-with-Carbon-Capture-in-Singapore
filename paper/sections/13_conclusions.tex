\section{Conclusions}
The proposed CN4252 solution is \textbf{nuclear-assisted SMR-H$_2$+CCS}. Its preferred future architecture uses one 600 MWth GTHTR300C-class high-temperature source, an intermediate/secondary-helium process-heat boundary and two 130 MMSCFD SMR-H$_2$+CCS trains requiring 353.6 MWth total reformer heat. The two-train architecture is project-derived from verified per-train source data; it is not a demonstrated JAEA/INL plant.

At the adopted 85\% basis, the preferred architecture produces approximately \textbf{195,892 tH$_2$/y} and the E2B lifecycle model gives approximately \textbf{1.834 MtCO$_2$e/y} avoided. This is a numerical PASS against the CN4252 abatement threshold. Economics are stage-dependent: the two-train FOAK case is approximately \textbf{S\$137.74/tCO$_2$e} and fails; the early-commercial/BOAK case is projected/modelled at approximately \textbf{S\$74.14/tCO$_2$e}; and the preferred mature 10-OAK case is projected/modelled at approximately \textbf{S\$42.84/tCO$_2$e}. The historical S\$3.725/t result is therefore not the controlling final economic answer.

The non-nuclear SMR+CCS comparator remains important. E2's matched-service screen gives approximately 0.461 MtCO$_2$e/y, showing that nuclear is not necessary merely to exceed 0.25 Mt/y. Nuclear remains in the proposed solution because replacing fired high-temperature reformer heat is the chosen novel intervention and because the two-train architecture tests whether higher real utilisation of a high-temperature source can support a larger decarbonisation system after credible maturation. The study does not establish nuclear as universally superior or as Singapore's definitive technology winner.

Deployment feasibility remains conditional. Jurong Island is a candidate industrial context, not an approved nuclear site. Source-technology safety evidence is supported and the integrated barrier/safe-state architecture is engineering-supported, but quantitative integrated safety is partial and licensing/site safety is not demonstrated. H$_2$ offtake, firm NG/water/cooling, approximately 1.085 MtCO$_2$/y permanent-storage capacity, project PRA/source term/QRA/separation, UHS/SBO, dose/EPR/EPZ, security, financing and a Singapore reactor-licensing pathway remain binding gates.

Accordingly, the current/FOAK project is \textbf{not deployment-feasible as demonstrated}, principally because FOAK economics fail and major deployment gates remain open. The preferred mature architecture is a \textbf{conditional future feasibility case}: it passes the CN4252 numerical thresholds in the model, but only measured replication, licensed site/design closure, contracts and operation can convert that projected result into demonstrated performance.
