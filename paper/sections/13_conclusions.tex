\section{Conclusions}
The final best-supported design is an INL/NGNP high-temperature nuclear-assisted SMR+CCS process using an 871$^\circ$C reformer, S/C=3.0, 900$^\circ$C delivered nuclear heat and a 925$^\circ$C HTGR process case, integrated with a 600 MWth GTHTR300C-class cogeneration module. INL's source Case 6 provides 130 MMSCFD H$_2$, 176.8 MWth process heat, 17.3 MWe process demand, 1,927 short ton/day captured CO$_2$ and 142 short ton/day emitted CO$_2$~\cite{inltev961}.

On the common INL conventional no-capture baseline, the final lifecycle model produces annual abatement well above the CN4252 0.25 MtCO$_2$e/y threshold. The forward economic result and its pass/fail classification are reported in Table~\ref{tab:final-result}; the conclusion is conditional on the declared GTHTR300C mature-design cost basis, enlarged IHX/secondary-loop cost treatment, CCS service assumptions and electricity offtake value.

Accordingly, the final submission does not claim demonstrated Singapore commercial feasibility. It establishes whether the strongest literature-supported nuclear-assisted configuration can satisfy the assignment thresholds under an explicit, reproducible set of engineering and economic assumptions. Historical 0/64 and 600$^\circ$C studies remain preserved as the audit trail rather than being presented as the operative design.
