\section{Conclusions}
The research question was whether a literature-anchored HTGR-assisted SMR+CCS configuration can supply the required reformer heat and satisfy the CN4252 screening thresholds. Within the declared model boundary, the answer is a \textbf{CONDITIONAL MODEL PASS}.

The thermal result closes first. INL Case 6 requires 176.8 MWth of reformer heat with a 925$^\circ$C reactor-outlet case, 900$^\circ$C secondary-helium supply and approximately 466$^\circ$C return at 78.49 kg/s~\cite{inltev961}. The selected GTHTR300C-class design basis is 600 MWth~\cite{nishihara2007potential}. Allocating 176.8 MWth to the reformer leaves 423.2 MWth of reactor thermal capacity. That value is \emph{not} project electricity output. The process duty is within the approximately 370/371 MWth source process-heat branch, but the branch is not one physical IHX; the 176.8 MWth project duty also remains a component-qualification question relative to the approximately 170 MWth reference physical-IHX evidence.

The source process produces 130 MMSCFD H$_2$~\cite{inltev953,inltev961}. Annualising the reported mass rate at the adopted 85\% design-study availability gives approximately 97,946 t H$_2$/y. The lifecycle ledger then gives approximately 862,094 t/y of direct CO$_2$ avoidance and approximately 917,139 tCO$_2$e/y of lifecycle avoidance, with a candidate intensity of approximately 1.95 kgCO$_2$e/kgH$_2$. The difference between direct and lifecycle avoidance follows from lower upstream natural-gas burden minus the added nuclear-heat, auxiliary-electricity and CO$_2$ transport/storage lifecycle terms.

The represented economic ledger gives a net incremental annual cost of approximately S\$3.416 million/y. Dividing that result by the lifecycle abatement gives approximately \textbf{S\$3.725/tCO$_2$e}. The calculation credits no project electricity revenue because no validated off-design project power-output model exists. The result therefore exceeds the CN4252 abatement requirement of 0.25 MtCO$_2$e/y and remains below the S\$100/tCO$_2$e ceiling under the adopted screening assumptions.

The qualification is as important as the pass. The calculation tests whether the proposed system closes on paper; it does not establish that a licensed plant can immediately be built in Singapore. Project-scale IHX/material qualification, coupled reactor--reformer transients, tritium control, mechanistic source term and PRA/EPZ analysis, nuclear/chemical QRA, siting and cooling, Singapore's nuclear regulatory framework, contracted cross-border CCS and bankable First-of-a-Kind economics remain unresolved deployment gates. The numerical result should therefore be used as a screening result that justifies deeper engineering, not as evidence of commercial or regulatory readiness.
