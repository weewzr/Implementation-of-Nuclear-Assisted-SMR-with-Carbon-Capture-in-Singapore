\section{Future Work: What Would Change the Decision?}
\label{sec:future-work}

The numerical CN4252 screen is complete; the deployment programme is not.  The remaining questions are not invitations to add generic references.  They require project-, site-, vendor- or regulator-specific analysis capable of moving a conclusion from \emph{conditional/unresolved} to either \emph{supported} or \emph{not supported}.

The next engineering milestone is therefore not a more favourable screening number.  It is evidence that closes the physical interfaces, accident/site consequences and real project-cost boundary while preserving the independently verified central bookkeeping.
