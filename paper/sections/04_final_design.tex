\section{Final literature-supported design and method}
\label{sec:final-design}

\subsection{Design selection}
The final process design is the INL/NGNP Case-6 HTGR-integrated SMR configuration with carbon capture, selected because it is the most directly relevant primary process analysis found for the proposed technology rather than because of its CN4252 outcome. INL selected a reformer outlet of 871$^\circ$C to balance methane conversion and tube life, steam/carbon ratio 3.0 to suppress carbon deposition, and 88\% PSA recovery~\cite{inltev953}. TEV-961 shows that a 925$^\circ$C HTGR outlet can supply 900$^\circ$C process heat and the entire reforming duty at the same 871$^\circ$C target, eliminating the fired second reformer~\cite{inltev961}. The corresponding hot-end process driving force is 29 K.

The final source balance is 130 MMSCFD H$_2$, 34.0 MMSCFD natural gas, 176.8 MWth nuclear process heat, 17.3 MWe process electricity, 1,927 short ton/day captured CO$_2$ and 142 short ton/day emitted CO$_2$~\cite{inltev961}. The same INL model family provides the conventional no-capture baseline at 130 MMSCFD H$_2$: 52.5 MMSCFD natural gas and 3,205 short ton/day emitted CO$_2$~\cite{inltev953}. This same-output comparison avoids mixing unrelated plant scales.

\subsection{Reactor and heat-delivery architecture}
The reactor/IHX hardware basis remains JAEA's 600 MWth GTHTR300C-class architecture and one-module cogeneration economics~\cite{jaeri2004gthtr300c,nishihara2007potential}. The final temperature ladder is:
\[
950^\circ\mathrm{C}\;\text{JAEA primary envelope}
\;>\;900^\circ\mathrm{C}\;\text{delivered process heat}
\;>\;871^\circ\mathrm{C}\;\text{reformer outlet}.
\]
INL's specific Case-6 reactor outlet is 925$^\circ$C; the 950$^\circ$C JAEA value is retained only as the selected hardware envelope. The required 176.8 MWth is below one 600 MWth module but slightly above JAEA's 170 MWth reference IHX. The final economic baseline therefore uses JAEA's doubled-IHX/secondary-loop cost sensitivity rather than pretending the 170 MWth reference exchanger is adequate.

\subsection{Lifecycle and deployment calculation}
Source direct emissions are annualized at the JAEA mature-design 85\% availability. Lifecycle accounting adds the difference in upstream natural-gas burden and subtracts nuclear-heat, incremental auxiliary-electricity and CO$_2$ transport/storage burdens. The upstream-gas factor (11.5 gCO$_2$e/MJ) is a global source proxy~\cite{iea2026lng}; the nuclear lifecycle proxy (5.5 gCO$_2$e/kWh) is global rather than Singapore-specific~\cite{unece2022lca}. The CCS transport burden remains an explicit screening assumption.

Annual lifecycle abatement is
\begin{equation}
A_{\rm avoided}=E_{\rm baseline}-E_{\rm candidate}.
\end{equation}
Only after this forward calculation are the CN4252 thresholds applied.

\subsection{Forward economics}
Review-5 economic corrections are retained. Historical JAEA and IEAGHG capital anchors are normalized to a common 2025-price basis before dated FX conversion. Full reactor heat and electricity economic burdens are charged; only net electricity remaining after the 17.3 MWe process demand receives an offtake credit. The final baseline uses the source doubled-IHX sensitivity (70.9 billion JPY, 0.57 JPY/MJ heat and 5.5 JPY/kWh electricity)~\cite{nishihara2007potential}. EMA's 2025 S\$100--200/MWh wholesale context defines the electricity-value sensitivity~\cite{emausep2025}. CCS capital is anchored to IEAGHG Case 1A and T\&S remains a clearly labelled Singapore screening scenario~\cite{ieaghg2017smr,mticcs2025cost}.

Forward abatement cost is
\begin{equation}
C_{\rm abatement}=\frac{C_{\rm candidate}-C_{\rm baseline}}{A_{\rm avoided}}.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Historical research boundary}
An earlier fixed-scale screening configuration tested 64 coupled cases and found no joint pass; a later 600$^\circ$C HTTR mock-up-based deployment state was also evaluated and superseded after broader literature synthesis. Both remain reproducible in the repository and review record, but neither is the operative final design.
