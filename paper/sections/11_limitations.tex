\section{Limitations}
The model is intentionally a screening model. Reforming and WGS use ideal-gas equilibrium rather than catalyst kinetics; the pre-reformer is a reduced stoichiometric representation. PSA is represented by a bounded recovery envelope rather than a bed-resolved adsorption cycle. The exact IEAGHG primary-reformer inlet is unpublished. Secondary-helium hydraulics are bounded rather than a detailed equipment/piping design. Economic prices, CAPEX allocations and T\&S remain scenario assumptions unless explicitly source-labelled. Upstream-gas, auxiliary-electricity and CCS-transport lifecycle factors are screening inputs rather than Singapore-specific measured inventories. The nuclear-heat lifecycle term is an allocation proxy obtained from a nuclear-electric lifecycle factor and assumed electric efficiency; it is not a published process-heat LCA. Some alternative pathways lack a fully matched Singapore forward-cost model. Nuclear deployment and cross-border CCS are future or conditional Singapore infrastructure.

These limitations restrict claim strength but do not invalidate the falsification result within the tested domain.
