\subsection{Model and deployment limitations}
The chemical model is a literature-anchored screening reconstruction, not a project-owned kinetic or detailed equipment simulation. INL source states anchor reforming/WGS/PSA/CCS performance; the two-train plant is a project-derived duplication of that per-train basis. Catalyst kinetics, detailed pressure drops, vessel sizing and dynamic plant control are not resolved.

The preferred economics are also deployment-stage models. FOAK/BOAK/10-OAK values use external cost/maturation methods and are not observed Singapore project quotations. Project-specific reformer modification, IHX/secondary-loop balance of plant, site/EPC/owner costs, licensing/security/EPR, waste/decommissioning/liability, financing/schedule and contracted CCS costs remain incomplete. No validated project electricity export or heat-sharing customer is credited.

The full process water and normal heat-rejection balance remains incomplete. E3 provides bounded cooling envelopes only; neither 246.4 nor 600 MWth is treated as canonical decay heat. A selected safety UHS requires a reactor decay-heat transient and site-specific architecture.

No Singapore nuclear site is selected. Jurong is only a candidate industrial context, with parcel-level land, geotechnical/coastal hazards, neighbouring industrial events, marine traffic, separation, security, emergency access and environmental acceptance unresolved. Cross-border permanent CO$_2$ storage is a binding dependency rather than a secured project service, and planned H$_2$ output has no assumed customer.

Licensing-level safety is not demonstrated. The project lacks a quantified PRA, project core radionuclide inventory, mechanistic source term, project IHX leak/rupture model, project tritium concentration, chemical risk contours, blast/fire separation distance, off-site dose and Singapore EPZ. HTTR, AGR and HTR-PM evidence supports mechanisms and methods but is not transferred as project accident performance.

Finally, comparator boundaries are not perfectly harmonised: E2's conventional SMR+CCS matched-service screen and E2B's preferred two-train INL-family scaling answer different attribution/architecture questions. They are used transparently for decision logic, not presented as a fully matched three-way LCA/TEA ranking.
