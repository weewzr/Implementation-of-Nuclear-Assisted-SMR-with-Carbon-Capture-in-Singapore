\section{Integrated feasibility and performance assessment}\label{sec:cn4252-assessment}

The originating study specified two numerical screening criteria: avoided lifecycle emissions must exceed 0.25 MtCO$_2$e/y and incremental abatement cost must remain below S\$100/tCO$_2$e. Meeting those numerical tests in a projected future scenario does not establish nuclear-site approval, plant safety or commercial readiness. The following assessment therefore reports a numerical result \emph{and} an evidence-maturity verdict.

\subsection{Controlling project result}
The proposed solution is \textbf{nuclear-assisted SMR-H$_2$+CCS}; conventional SMR+CCS is the non-nuclear comparator/counterfactual. The preferred future architecture is one 600 MWth GTHTR300C-class source serving two 130 MMSCFD SMR-H$_2$+CCS trains through primary helium, an IHX/secondary-helium boundary and 353.6 MWth total reformer duty.

At 85\% availability the project-derived two-train scale is approximately \textbf{195,892 tH$_2$/y}. E2B retains the verified per-train lifecycle boundary and duplicates the identical project train, rather than mixing it with the separate E2 comparator denominator. In compact form,
\[
A_{\rm lifecycle,2train}
=2A_{\rm lifecycle,train}
=2(917{,}139)
\approx1.834278\times10^6~\mathrm{tCO_2e/y}.
\]
The per-train lifecycle result contains the direct-emissions saving and upstream-NG saving, less added nuclear-heat, auxiliary-electricity and CO$_2$ transport/storage lifecycle burdens. Captured CO$_2$ is not added again to the avoided-emissions numerator because its effect is already reflected in the source-reported candidate residual emissions and the explicit T\&S burden. Thus the preferred E2B architecture avoids approximately \textbf{1.834 MtCO$_2$e/y} on its declared boundary, exceeding 0.25 Mt/y; this is a project-model result, not measured operation.

\subsection{Comparator and nuclear attribution}
E2 separately tested a matched-service conventional SMR+CCS comparator and obtained approximately \textbf{0.461 MtCO$_2$e/y} abatement. That cross-source comparator boundary is not identical to the E2B two-train scaling and is therefore not merged arithmetically with the preferred result. It establishes an important decision point: CCS alone can exceed the minimum CN4252 abatement threshold. Nuclear is therefore not claimed to be necessary merely for minimum compliance.

Nuclear process heat remains the project's chosen intervention because it replaces fired high-temperature reformer heat, creates the primary-He$\rightarrow$IHX$\rightarrow$secondary-He integration problem and enables the investigated high-utilisation two-train architecture. The project must justify that additional complexity rather than assume nuclear superiority.

\subsection{Deployment-stage economics}
Table~\ref{tab:deployment-economics} gives the controlling economics. FOAK is approximately S\$137.74/tCO$_2$e and \textbf{fails} the S\$100/t threshold. Early-commercial/BOAK is projected/modelled at approximately S\$74.14/tCO$_2$e. The preferred mature 10-OAK case is projected/modelled at approximately S\$42.84/tCO$_2$e. The historical S\$3.725/t screening bridge is superseded as a controlling result.

\subsection{Singapore/Jurong and safety status}
Jurong Island is classified as a \textbf{conditional candidate industrial context; nuclear-site feasibility is not demonstrated}. The two-train architecture requires 68 MMSCFD NG, 260 MMSCFD H$_2$ product handling and approximately 1.085 MtCO$_2$/y captured-CO$_2$ transport/storage. E3 shows that coastal cooling options can be screened quantitatively, but the 246.4 MWth full-power residual identity is not the canonical operating cooling duty and neither 600 MWth nor 246.4 MWth is a decay-heat duty.

Safety evidence is deliberately hierarchical:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{source technology: SUPPORTED} by relevant HTTR/AGR/JAEA evidence;
\item \textbf{project safety architecture: ENGINEERING-SUPPORTED} through barriers, safe states and bidirectional nuclear/chemical propagation logic;
\item \textbf{quantitative integrated safety: CONDITIONAL/PARTIAL};
\item \textbf{licensing/site safety: NOT DEMONSTRATED}.
\end{itemize}
Project PRA frequencies, mechanistic source term, chemical risk contours, separation distance, off-site dose and EPZ are not invented. Required analyses include 600 MWth coupled transients, PRA/reliability, IHX failure, tritium, chemical HAZOP/QRA, UHS/SBO, Jurong external hazards, dispersion/dose, EPR/EPZ and security.
