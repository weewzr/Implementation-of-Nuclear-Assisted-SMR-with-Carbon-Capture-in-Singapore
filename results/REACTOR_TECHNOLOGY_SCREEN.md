## Application-specific conclusion

The project does **not** conclude that HTGRs are generally superior to LWRs.

It concludes that for **direct ~900 C-class process heat to an SMR reformer**, representative water-cooled reactors do not supply the required temperature directly and need a temperature-augmentation architecture.

HTTR proves the temperature regime but not the scale.

HTR-PM provides the strongest current operating modular-HTGR evidence for fuel, safety, licensing, emergency planning, helium systems and human factors, but its operating steam-cycle plant is not the direct 900 C secondary-helium reformer configuration.

GTHTR300C is retained because its published design uniquely matches the project's **temperature + process-heat architecture + scale** filters among the screened families.

The selection remains conditional on large-IHX qualification, tritium/radiological interface, reliability, site/safety and fuel-supply gates.
