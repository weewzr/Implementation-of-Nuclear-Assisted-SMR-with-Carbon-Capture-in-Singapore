## Primary source recovered
IEAGHG, *Techno-Economic Evaluation of SMR Based Standalone (Merchant) Hydrogen Plant with CCS*, Report 2017-02 (February 2017), full report:
https://publications.ieaghg.org/technicalreports/2017-02%20Techno%20-%20Economic%20Evaluation%20of%20SMR%20Based%20Standalone%20%28Merchant%29%20Hydrogen%20Plant%20with%20CCS.pdf

Official overview: https://ieaghg.org/publications/techno-economic-evaluation-of-smr-based-standalone-merchant-hydrogen-plant-with-ccs/

Related primary open-access technical paper: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.egypro.2017.03.1533

IEAGHG's study evaluates a **100,000 Nm3/h H2 merchant plant**, with capture from (i) shifted syngas, (ii) PSA tail gas, or (iii) SMR furnace flue gas. It reports overall capture rates across configurations of roughly **53–90%**, incremental total plant capital of **€40–176 million in Q4 2014** (18–79% over baseline), operating-cost increases of **18–33%**, and CO2 avoidance costs **€47–70/t**. These are **alternative plant configurations**, not directly additive costs or the proposed nuclear-heated Case-6 train. The study's baseline includes feed pretreatment, pre-reformer, primary reformer, shift and PSA. It also credits electricity export in its own configurations; **no electricity export is credited in this project**.

Related IEAGHG review 2017-TR3:
https://ieaghg.org/publications/reference-data-and-supporting-literature-reviews-for-smr-based-hydrogen-production-with-ccs/
This documents PSA-tail-gas capture examples (Tomakomai amine capture and Port-Jerome cryogenic/membrane treatment). **Those examples do not establish the fate of INL Case-6 PSA offgas.**

## Key scientific contradiction exposed
The IEAGHG review explicitly distinguishes **90% removal from a particular stream** from **overall plant capture**. It reports that shifted syngas and PSA tail-gas capture alone often capture only about 55–60% of total SMR emissions, whereas flue-gas capture can approach 90% overall (configuration dependent). Consequently, a generic 90% absorber efficiency cannot be assigned as a 90% plant-wide figure. This reinforces the existing PSA closure gate.
