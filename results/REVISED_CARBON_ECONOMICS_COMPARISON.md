## Decision
Do not silently replace the E2B point estimates with invented revised central values. The old results remain provenance for the earlier screening model; the **current claim of a fully verified carbon and economic PASS is suspended** pending the two scientific gates: streamwise elemental carbon closure and complete matched-service annualized cost bounds.

## Quantitative sensitivity contract
Once inputs exist, vary PSA tail-gas carbon partition (recycle/combustion/export/purge), absorber-inlet CO2 capture efficiency, CO2 transport/storage cost, chemical CAPEX/OPEX and nuclear maturity. Recompute emissions and cost under equal hydrogen service; reject scenarios whose carbon balance residual exceeds a stated tolerance. Do not treat changing PSA offgas fate as a free CO2 credit or double count captured CO2.

## Evidence requests
Original INL TEV-953 and TEV-961 stream tables/PFD, source-model PSA offgas composition and destination, absorber inlet/outlet and flue-gas carbon flows; matched-scope NETL/IEAGHG chemical plant and CCS cost estimates; Singapore integration, licensing and transport/storage pricing. Until these are obtained, neither a numerical plant-wide capture efficiency nor fully priced FOAK/BOAK/10-OAK result is defensible.
