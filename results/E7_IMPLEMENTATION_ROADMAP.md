# E7 Decision-Gated Implementation Roadmap

## 1. Purpose and project identity

This roadmap implements the proposed **NUCLEAR-ASSISTED SMR-H2 + CCS** project:
**1 x 600 MWth GTHTR300C-class source -> 353.6 MWth process heat -> 2 x 130 MMSCFD SMR-H2+CCS trains.**

Conventional SMR+CCS remains the non-nuclear comparator/counterfactual. It is not substituted into the roadmap because it is easier to deploy.

The roadmap is a falsification pathway. Every stage must produce measurable evidence and has a REDESIGN / RELOCATE / REDUCE SCALE / DELAY / STOP outcome where appropriate.

No arbitrary calendar dates are assigned.

## 2. Submission-facing roadmap skeleton

**CURRENT RESEARCH**
-> **COMPONENT / INTEGRATION QUALIFICATION**
-> **FOAK PROCESS-HEAT DEMONSTRATION**
-> **TWO-TRAIN 353.6 MWth DEMONSTRATION**
-> **REPLICATION / EARLY-COMMERCIAL BOAK**
-> **MATURE / 10-OAK COST GATE**
-> **SINGAPORE SITE + LICENSING + CONTRACT CLOSURE**
-> **CONSTRUCTION / COMMISSIONING**
-> **COMMERCIAL OPERATION / MEASURED VERIFICATION**

Cross-cutting workstreams for regulation, safety, site, cooling, H2, NG, CCS, environment/EPR/security and supply chain progress in parallel where dependencies permit, but must converge before FID.

## 3. Stage 0 — Current research / pre-deployment

Current facts:
- Singapore nuclear deployment decision/project licensing path is not established for this project;
- Jurong is only a conditional candidate industrial context;
- FOAK economics fail: ~S$137.74/tCO2e for the two-train architecture;
- licensing-level integrated safety is not demonstrated;
- H2 offtake and ~1.085 MtCO2/y storage chain are not secured.

**Gate:** maintain the architecture only as a research/pre-development proposition until national, technology, site, safety and commercial evidence advances.

## 4. Singapore national-readiness gate

Before real project development, Singapore requires an applicable power-reactor framework: national policy path, competent independent regulator, legal/licensing system, safeguards/security, waste/spent-fuel policy, emergency preparedness, liability/insurance and qualified workforce/supply chain, with IAEA newcomer/INIR progression as applicable.

Current radiation regulation is not treated as a complete reactor licensing regime.

**GO:** competent authorities provide a sufficiently defined pathway for reactor project/site licensing.
**NO-GO:** no policy/legal/regulatory pathway -> Singapore deployment cannot advance; technology R&D may continue.

## 5. Stage 1 — Technology qualification

Required evidence:
- selected reactor/fuel design basis;
- validated 600 MWth coupled thermal-hydraulic/control model;
- single- and two-train process-load-loss response;
- decay-heat/UHS architecture;
- high-temperature IHX structural/thermal/creep-fatigue qualification;
- secondary-He circulation/isolation;
- tritium/permeation and purification;
- reformer heat-transfer/material qualification;
- controls/load following;
- inspection/maintenance strategy.

**GO:** required temperature/duty and safe-state margins are demonstrated under applicable design/regulatory requirements.
**FAIL:** redesign interface/material/control, reduce scale, or stop the architecture.

## 6. Integrated safety gate

E4/E5 requirements become binding analyses:
PRA; mechanistic source term; IHX leak/rupture; tritium; chemical HAZOP/QRA; blast/fire/toxic effects; bidirectional propagation; common cause; UHS/SBO; site dispersion/dose; EPR/EPZ; physical/cyber security.

Where Singapore has no established project numerical criterion, the gate is: **must satisfy the applicable future Singapore regulator plus IAEA/design-basis requirements**.

Safety failure may require barriers, separation, redesign, relocation, scale reduction or stop.

## 7. Stage 2 — FOAK process-heat demonstration

FOAK must demonstrate the nuclear intervention physically: high-temperature heat passes through a qualified IHX/secondary-He system into reformer-relevant service with measured heat/temperature/control/isolation/tritium/material performance.

FOAK economics are allowed to remain above the CN4252 threshold; the current model is ~S$137.74/tCO2e.

**GO:** stable, qualified high-temperature service and safe trip/load-rejection response.
**FAIL:** redesign or stop direct coupling.

## 8. Stage 3 — Two-train architecture demonstration

This is the central architecture gate, not an accounting allocation.

Demonstrate:
- **353.6 MWth** useful process heat;
- operation within the source ~370 MW process-heat branch;
- required temperature approach;
- load sharing;
- one-train trip;
- simultaneous two-train trip;
- reactor safe response;
- turndown/restart;
- CCS interaction;
- H2 quality.

**GO:** both trains meet service and safety requirements without exceeding source/design limits.
**FAIL:** redesign controls/manifold, reduce scale or abandon the preferred two-train economics.

## 9. Availability gate

The 85% model basis is an **integrated service** assumption, not reactor availability alone.

Measure joint availability of reactor + IHX/secondary loop + both H2 trains + CCS + CO2 transport/storage.

If actual integrated availability differs materially, recalculate annual H2, abatement and S$/tCO2e.

**GO:** demonstrated availability supports the model basis or the revised case still meets decision criteria.
**FAIL:** improve reliability/redundancy or reject the economic case.

## 10. Stage 4 — Replication / early-commercial BOAK

Replicate a standardised demonstrated design and record:
- actual OCC/CAPEX;
- construction duration;
- labour/productivity;
- supply-chain maturity;
- O&M;
- availability;
- integration cost;
- CCS/T&S;
- site/licensing/security costs.

E2B's ~S$74.14/tCO2e is a **projected/modelled target**, not an entitlement.

**GO:** updated total represented cost remains <S$100/tCO2e.
**FAIL:** do not advance merely because learning was expected.

## 11. Stage 5 — Mature / 10-OAK economic gate

At repeated comparable builds, compare observed learning against the INL/DOE evidence basis.

E2B's ~S$42.84/tCO2e and ~S$104.84m/y represented margin are **targets/tolerance**, not free money.

The mature result can be treated as more than a modelled target only after actual replication provides cost, O&M, construction-time and availability evidence and the forward project calculation includes then-known integration, site, licensing, security, CCS and other project costs.

**GO:** recalculated represented cost remains <S$100/tCO2e.
**NO-GO:** if actual mature economics remain >=S$100/t, the CN4252 mature economic PASS is rejected unless the design is materially changed and re-evaluated.

## 12. Candidate-site / Jurong gate

Jurong remains a candidate industrial context, not the selected site.

Required parcel work:
geotechnical/seismic; coastal/flood/storm-surge; neighbour fire/explosion/toxic hazards; pipelines/storage; shipping; footprint; QRA/PRA-derived nuclear/chemical separation; protected area/security; construction logistics; emergency access; cooling intake/outfall/alternative; environmental/radiological baseline.

**GO:** parcel passes regulator/site evaluation and integrated QRA/PRA/environmental requirements.
**JURONG NO-GO:** evaluate another Singapore context.
**TECHNOLOGY NO-GO:** only if no Singapore context can satisfy binding siting/safety/security/EPR constraints.

## 13. Cooling and water gate

Close:
- complete reactor/process/CCS heat balance;
- normal heat rejection;
- decay-heat/UHS duty;
- selected cooling architecture;
- intake/outfall/tower;
- thermal plume/ecology;
- blockage/fouling/corrosion;
- flood/coastal resilience;
- process/makeup water.

Neither 246.4 nor 600 MWth is the canonical cooling/decay-heat duty.

**GO:** normal and safety heat sinks close with acceptable environmental/safety margins.
**FAIL:** redesign cooling/operating point or relocate.

## 14. H2 offtake gate

Scale: **260 MMSCFD / ~195,892 tH2/y**.

Require identified users, pressure/purity, pipeline/storage/buffer, turndown/curtailment and commercial basis.

**GO before FID:** credible/contracted offtake sufficient for planned scale.
**FAIL:** reduce scale, delay or stop commercial project.

## 15. NG/feedstock gate

Scale: **68 MMSCFD**, ~**21.1 Bscf/y at 85% availability**.

Require firm capacity, pressure/connection, redundancy, resilience and acceptable upstream/lifecycle performance.

The nuclear-assisted project remains methane-dependent.

## 16. CCS gate

Scale: approximately **1.085 MtCO2/y captured**.

Require qualified capture/conditioning, buffer, transport mode/terminal/pipeline/shipping, contracted cross-border transport and permanent storage, MRV/accounting, liability and regulatory recognition.

MOUs are not storage contracts.

**GO:** contracted project-scale chain with cost/lifecycle burden compatible with economics.
**FAIL:** delay, reduce production, secure alternative storage, or stop the claimed CCS abatement architecture.

## 17. Stage 6 — Singapore project development / commercial / licensing closure

### Financing/commercial
Current screening economics are not bankable finance. Develop project CAPEX/TCI, schedule, financing/IDC, insurance/liability, decommissioning/waste, revenues/offtake, CCS contracts and owner/EPC costs.

### Environmental/social/EPR
Complete marine/ecological, thermal, radiological, industrial, transport, waste and emergency-planning assessments and applicable approval/consultation processes. No unsupported public-acceptance claim is made.

### Final investment/licensing
Before FID/construction require:
- national policy allows project;
- site approved;
- design licensed;
- safety case accepted;
- environmental approvals;
- H2/NG/CCS/cooling/water contracts/infrastructure;
- security/EPR;
- finance;
- economics still meet the project's decision criterion.

Failure of a binding gate -> redesign, delay, relocate or stop.

## 18. Stage 7 — Construction and commissioning

Only after FID/licensing closure:
- construct under nuclear/chemical QA;
- verify as-built configuration;
- commission reactor/IHX/secondary loop;
- commission both H2/CCS trains;
- test trips/isolation/safety systems and interfaces.

**GO:** licensed acceptance and contractual performance tests passed.
**FAIL:** rework/retest/redesign; no commercial operation.

## 19. Stage 8 — Commercial operation and verification

Measure:
- reactor thermal performance;
- IHX/secondary-loop performance;
- 353.6 MWth two-train service;
- H2 production/quality;
- capture and permanent CO2 delivery;
- lifecycle emissions;
- integrated availability;
- safety-system performance;
- actual operating/project cost.

Only measured operation can convert projected mature performance into demonstrated performance. Claims must be recalculated if actual performance differs.

## 20. Critical path

The critical path is not one purely serial engineering line. Key dependencies are:
1. national regulatory readiness -> formal site/licensing;
2. design definition -> coupled transient/PRA/source term/QRA;
3. component qualification -> FOAK;
4. FOAK -> two-train demonstration;
5. two-train demonstration -> standardised replication;
6. replication -> observed BOAK/10-OAK learning;
7. source term + parcel meteorology -> dose/EPR/EPZ;
8. QRA/PRA -> final separation/layout;
9. parcel -> final cooling/environmental design;
10. H2 + NG + CCS + approvals + finance -> FID;
11. FID -> construction -> commissioning -> measured operation.

## 21. Parallel workstreams

Legitimate parallel work before convergence:
- reactor/IHX/component R&D;
- Singapore regulatory capability building;
- CCS commercial development;
- H2 market/offtake;
- candidate-site screening;
- safety modelling as design/site data mature;
- supply-chain/workforce development;
- economic-data collection from FOAK/replication.

Parallel work does not waive downstream dependencies.

## 22. Roadmap falsification logic

The canonical stop register is `results/e7_roadmap/e7_stop_conditions.csv`.

The project does not advance automatically. Examples:
- unsafe/unqualifiable heat interface -> redesign/stop;
- two-train dynamics fail -> reduce scale/redesign;
- mature economics remain >=S$100/t -> reject mature economic PASS;
- no separation/UHS/site/EPR solution -> redesign/relocate/stop;
- no H2 market or CCS chain -> reduce/delay/stop;
- regulator rejects design/site -> stop/delay/redesign;
- commissioning fails licensed criteria -> do not operate.

The roadmap explicitly permits rejection of nuclear integration if its incremental value cannot justify its complexity. That decision does not turn this project's proposed solution into conventional SMR+CCS; it means the nuclear-assisted proposal failed its own deployment gates.

## 23. E7 closure

E7 converts E2B-E6 unresolved items into measurable implementation gates. It does not promise deployment or invent dates.

**Current state remains pre-deployment research. The next maturity transition is technology/integration qualification, not construction.**
