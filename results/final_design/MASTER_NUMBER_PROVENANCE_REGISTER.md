## Audit dispositions

- **No material scientific contradiction found in the frozen central arithmetic.**
- The most important newly exposed provenance weakness is **A-01 = 6.3 MWe**: it is retained from the historical project model as a screening common/baseline electricity anchor, but the final repository does not establish it as an INL Case-6/common-output source value. The manuscript must label it as such rather than allowing it to appear source-derived.
- A-03 (1044 Btu/scf), A-07 (0.025 tCO2e/tCO2), A-08 (S$15/GJ), A-14 (10%), and A-16 (S$15/tCO2) are screening assumptions/bases and must not be presented as measured Singapore/project values.
- D-03 through D-21 are project-derived outputs; citations belong on their source inputs, not falsely on the derived result itself.
