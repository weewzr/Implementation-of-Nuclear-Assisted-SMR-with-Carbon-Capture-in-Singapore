# Final report foundation reconciliation

## Original research foundation recovered
Sources inspected: `PROJECT_BRIEF.md`, `RESEARCH_FRAMING.md`, the current manuscript and canonical E2B sizing/cost model, plus the repository attachment/data/literature inventory. The original hypothesis was to replace fired reformer heat with HTGR heat while retaining steam methane reforming and amine CCS, potentially removing an electricity-conversion path. The original comparison matrix included fired SMR, fired SMR+CCS, eSMR, nuclear-electric eSMR, direct HTGR-heated SMR+CCS and electrolysis. It expressly required falsification of the claim that nuclear is needed, and attention to methane leakage, heat temperature, Singapore siting and CCS logistics.

## Manuscript identity and provenance
The main title/author/abstract/introduction/assessment/conclusions were revised to read as an independent engineering research paper rather than a CN4252 submission. The originating numerical criteria (>0.25 MtCO2e/y, <S$100/tCO2e) are preserved as **academic screening criteria** in the design-basis methodology and appendix, not as regulatory standards.

## Remaining gaps
The manuscript still includes detailed historical one-train calculations and figures for provenance; a future focused layout/editorial pass may move some to a technical appendix, but no scientific records were deleted. Full competing-technology comparison, complete chemical plant CAPEX/OPEX, site approval, quantified integrated safety, IHX qualification and contracted CCS remain unresolved. No claim of global technology optimality is supported.
