# E6 CN4252 Feasibility, Effectiveness and Requirement Synthesis

> **Project identity:** The proposed CN4252 solution is **nuclear-assisted SMR-H2 + CCS**. Conventional SMR-H2 + CCS is the **non-nuclear comparator/counterfactual alternative**, not a replacement proposed solution. E2's comparator result is retained to test the incremental value and justification of nuclear integration.

## 1. Executive decision

The project now has two distinct deployment conclusions that must not be collapsed.

**Current / FOAK:** the proposed nuclear-assisted route is **NOT CURRENTLY COMMERCIALLY OR DEPLOYMENT FEASIBLE AS DEMONSTRATED**. The stronger E1 one-module case is ~S$154/tCO2e and the E2B two-train FOAK case is ~S$137.74/tCO2e, both above the CN4252 S$100/t threshold. Singapore has not decided to deploy nuclear power; Jurong is not an approved nuclear site; integrated licensing-level safety is not demonstrated; and the CO2 transport/storage chain is not secured.

**Preferred mature / future:** one 600 MWth GTHTR300C-class reactor serving two 130 MMSCFD SMR-H2+CCS trains is a **CONDITIONAL FUTURE FEASIBILITY CASE**. It uses 353.6 MWth process heat, produces ~195,892 tH2/y, abates ~1.834 MtCO2e/y on the E2B scaling basis, and has projected/modelled economics of ~S$74.14/tCO2e at early-commercial/BOAK and ~S$42.84/tCO2e at preferred mature 10-OAK. Those values are not current quotations or observed Singapore project costs.

## 2. CN4252 assignment spine

### A. Decarbonisation problem and context
Singapore industrial hydrogen made by conventional natural-gas steam methane reforming produces CO2 because methane supplies both hydrogen feedstock and fired process heat. CCS can capture a large fraction of process CO2, while replacing fired reformer heat with high-temperature nuclear process heat can further reduce gas use and direct combustion emissions.

Singapore/Jurong matters because implementation requires industrial NG, hydrogen offtake, water/steam, cooling, CO2 conditioning/export, hazardous-industry integration and a nuclear regulatory/siting framework.

### B. Proposed solution
The proposed CN4252 solution is **nuclear-assisted SMR-H2 + CCS**. Its novel intervention is high-temperature HTGR process heat integrated through primary helium -> IHX -> secondary helium -> reformers. Conventional SMR+CCS remains the non-nuclear comparator/counterfactual used to test whether the added nuclear complexity is justified.

Preferred future architecture:
- one 600 MWth GTHTR300C-class high-temperature reactor;
- primary helium -> IHX -> secondary helium;
- two 130 MMSCFD SMR-H2+amine-CCS trains;
- 353.6 MWth total useful reformer/process heat;
- no electricity revenue or unsupported co-product credit.

### C. Abatement potential
Preferred E2B result: **~1.834 MtCO2e/y lifecycle abatement**, versus the official **>0.25 MtCO2e/y** threshold. Numerically this is a strong modelled PASS.

Boundary warning: E2's matched-service cross-source comparator and E2B's canonical INL-family scaling are not identical denominators and must not be silently mixed.

### D. Cost
The controlling stage-specific economic story is:
- historical S$3.725/tCO2e: superseded differential screening bridge; audit history only;
- E1 stronger one-module central: **~S$154/tCO2e — FAIL**;
- E2B two-train FOAK: **~S$137.74/tCO2e — FAIL**;
- E2B early-commercial/BOAK: **~S$74.14/tCO2e — projected/modelled PASS**;
- E2B preferred mature 10-OAK: **~S$42.84/tCO2e — projected/modelled PASS**.

The future passes depend on maturation, standardisation and unresolved project/site costs not invalidating the model.

### E. Key questions
The remaining questions are specific deployment gates: coupled transients, commercial IHX qualification, PRA/source term/tritium, chemical QRA and separation, candidate parcel/external hazards, UHS/cooling, H2 offtake, >=~1.085 Mt/y CO2 transport/storage, dose/EPR/EPZ, security and Singapore licensing.

## 3. Why nuclear if CCS alone can pass?

E2 is decisive: conventional SMR+CCS alone gives approximately **0.461 MtCO2e/y** on its matched-service screen, already above 0.25 Mt/y. Therefore nuclear is **not necessary merely to satisfy the CN4252 abatement threshold**.

Nuclear heat changes the physical process by replacing fired high-temperature reformer duty and reducing natural-gas combustion/feed requirements relative to the nuclear-assisted source design. E2B then uses one 600 MWth source more effectively by serving two reformer trains, producing a larger ~195,892 tH2/y platform and ~1.834 MtCO2e/y modelled abatement.

What nuclear does not uniquely provide:
- CCS itself;
- the minimum 0.25 Mt/y compliance;
- guaranteed low-cost hydrogen;
- a guaranteed Singapore deployment pathway.

The defensible reason to study nuclear is therefore not threshold necessity. **Nuclear integration is the project's chosen novel intervention and proposed solution:** it replaces fired high-temperature reformer heat, introduces the primary-He -> IHX -> secondary-He physical integration, enables the investigated high-utilisation two-train architecture, and tests whether that larger nuclear-assisted decarbonisation system can become economically viable after credible deployment maturation. Conventional SMR+CCS is retained as the non-nuclear comparator against which that additional complexity must be justified. The evidence does not establish nuclear as universally superior or as Singapore's definitive technology winner.

## 4. Potential effectiveness

At the preferred future scale the concept is potentially material: ~195,892 tH2/y and ~1.834 MtCO2e/y lifecycle abatement on the E2B basis.

Effectiveness depends on:
- ~85% availability;
- sustained two-train operation;
- 68 MMSCFD NG supply;
- 260 MMSCFD H2 offtake/product handling;
- ~1.085 MtCO2/y capture, transport and permanent storage;
- actual displacement of fired reformer heat;
- nuclear/IHX availability and safe integration;
- cooling/water/power support;
- cost maturation.

Real-world abatement falls if availability falls, capture/storage is interrupted, H2 lacks offtake, backup firing rises, or lifecycle/infrastructure burdens exceed the model. Economic effectiveness falls if nuclear learning/standardisation or unresolved integration/site costs do not mature as projected.

## 5. Singapore and Jurong feasibility

**Industrial integration:** SUPPORTED as a candidate context; Jurong has relevant E&C infrastructure.

**Land/footprint:** UNRESOLVED at parcel level. Island-scale land does not prove a nuclear-compatible parcel.

**NG:** industrial access is plausible; project firm capacity remains to be contracted/engineered.

**H2 offtake:** required at 260 MMSCFD / ~195,892 t/y; not secured.

**CCS:** ~1.085 Mt/y captured CO2 requires conditioning, transport and permanent storage. Geological storage may be cross-border and remains a deployment dependency.

**Cooling/water:** options exist, but exact normal heat rejection and nuclear UHS remain design-specific.

**Nuclear/chemical separation:** no generic distance is defensible; QRA/PRA/consequence analysis must determine layout.

**External hazards:** Jurong refinery/chemical/marine/flood hazards are major unresolved siting inputs.

**Security/EPR/environment/licensing:** not demonstrated.

Therefore:
**JURONG ISLAND = CONDITIONAL CANDIDATE INDUSTRIAL CONTEXT / NUCLEAR SITE SUITABILITY NOT DEMONSTRATED.**

"Within Singapore" is interpreted as Singapore-based H2 production and principal industrial integration, with emissions avoided relative to a Singapore industrial counterfactual and CO2 captured/conditioned in Singapore. Permanent geological storage may occur cross-border; that external storage dependency must remain explicit.

## 6. Safety synthesis

**SUPPORTED:** HTTR/AGR/JAEA provide relevant experimental/operational source-technology evidence.

**ENGINEERING-SUPPORTED:** E4 establishes barriers, safe states and bidirectional nuclear/chemical propagation logic.

**CONDITIONAL/PARTIAL:** E5 structures quantitative event trees, source-term/QRA chains and limited defensible quantitative screens.

**NOT DEMONSTRATED:** licensing-level integrated project/site safety.

Decision-critical unresolved work includes 600 MWth coupled transients, PRA/reliability, mechanistic source term, IHX leak/rupture, tritium, chemical QRA, blast/fire separation, Jurong external hazards, UHS/SBO, site dispersion/dose, EPR/EPZ and security/regulatory acceptance.

Any of these can still force redesign, reject a parcel, alter economics/classification, or falsify deployment feasibility.

## 7. Originality

The originality is not any individual technology: nuclear hydrogen, HTGRs, SMR-H2, CCS and amine capture all have prior art. Existing Singapore reforming/CCS initiatives strengthen the need to distinguish this project from an ordinary SMR+CCS proposal; they do not redefine the proposed solution as conventional CCS.

Project-specific originality is the **Singapore-focused integration and falsifiable engineering assessment** of:
- high-temperature nuclear process heat replacing fired reformer heat;
- integration with SMR-H2 + amine CCS;
- one-source/two-train utilisation architecture;
- Singapore/Jurong-specific siting/infrastructure analysis;
- integrated nuclear/chemical safety treatment;
- stage-specific FOAK -> mature economic falsification/recovery pathway.

This is a distinct engineering integration and deployment-feasibility question rather than a claim that its individual technologies are novel.

## 8. Comparator decision logic

### Conventional SMR
Reference high-emissions hydrogen route. No CCS and no nuclear integration.

### SMR + CCS — non-nuclear comparator/counterfactual
E2 matched-service screen gives ~0.461 MtCO2e/y, enough to clear the assignment's minimum abatement threshold. This comparator result tests the incremental justification for adding nuclear; it does **not** replace nuclear-assisted SMR-H2+CCS as the proposed CN4252 solution.

### Mature nuclear-assisted SMR + CCS
Adds fired-heat displacement and a larger two-train architecture. E2B gives ~1.834 MtCO2e/y and projected mature economics below S$100/t, but adds nuclear capital, licensing, IHX, safety, siting and common-cause complexity.

The E2 and E2B boundaries are not perfectly matched, so this is decision logic rather than a claim of a fully harmonised three-way LCA/TEA ranking.

## 10. What the research falsified

The research has falsified or superseded:
1. the idea that an under-utilised one-train 600 MWth nuclear-assisted architecture is robustly economical;
2. S$3.725/tCO2e as the controlling final economic interpretation;
3. the assumption that nuclear is necessary to exceed 0.25 MtCO2e/y;
4. any claim that Jurong is already demonstrated as a feasible nuclear site;
5. any claim that generic HTGR/TRISO literature proves the integrated project is safe;
6. an unconditional present-tense claim that the project meets the <S$100/t threshold.

What remains viable is a forward-designed one-reactor/two-train architecture whose numerical threshold performance is promising at projected mature cost, conditional on explicit engineering, safety, site, CCS, market and regulatory gates.
