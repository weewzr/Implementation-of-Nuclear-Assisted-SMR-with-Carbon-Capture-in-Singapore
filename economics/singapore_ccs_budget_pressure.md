# Singapore CCS transport/storage budget pressure

## Authoritative assignment target

The official CN4252 Problem Statement in Project Sources requires:
- >0.25 MtCO2e/y within Singapore;
- <S$100/tCO2e.

This remains the controlling target even though current public programmes may publish different thresholds.

## Singapore-specific T&S evidence

An IEAGHG 2023 cost-network workshop contains an ExxonMobil analysis specifically titled:

*South East Asia CO2 Transport and Storage Cost Estimates for CO2 Sources in Singapore.*

Its cost curve groups opportunities approximately as:
- Group A: USD50-75/tCO2;
- Group B: USD75-150/tCO2;
- Group C: USD150-450/tCO2.

The chart includes subsurface storage, offshore transport, compression/dehydration, onshore transport, offshore platform, liquefaction/dehydration and loading-terminal components.

These are **study estimates**, not contracted tariffs or a current Singapore government price.

MTI stated in April 2025 that Singapore was still working with potential service providers to obtain clearer estimates for the complete CCS value chain. EMA similarly notes relatively high transport/storage cost and risk.

## First budget-pressure calculation

For the current 90%-capture screening case, captured feedstock carbon is about:

~6.95 kgCO2/kgH2.

At 71.952 ktH2/y this is about:

~0.50 MtCO2/y captured.

Use a representative late-September-2026 FX sensitivity of:

1 USD ~= 1.276 SGD.

Then Group-A T&S alone would cost approximately:

USD50/t -> S$31.9m/y
USD75/t -> S$47.9m/y

for 0.50 Mt/y captured.

The illustrative assignment-compatible incremental-cost budget at 7.5 kgCO2e/kgH2 avoided is about:

S$54m/y.

Therefore Group-A-like T&S alone consumes approximately:
- ~59% of that annual budget at USD50/t;
- ~89% at USD75/t.

Residual budget for **all** incremental capture, nuclear, IHX/loop, tail-gas, financing and O&M costs would then be only approximately:

- S$22m/y at USD50/t;
- S$6m/y at USD75/t.

This is a screening calculation. The exact captured mass, lifecycle abatement, FX and T&S tariff will change.

## Economic implication

The CN4252 S$100/tCO2e target appears much more restrictive than the physical >0.25 Mt/y target.

If Singapore cross-border T&S remains near the published Group-A range, the proposed architecture may require:
- very low incremental capture cost;
- strong cogeneration/shared-reactor cost allocation;
- unusually low-cost storage/transport;
- avoided-cost/carbon-tax credits;
- or a lower-emission/higher-abatement counterfactual structure.

Even cogeneration cannot make high T&S cost disappear.

## Important distinction

The T&S chart is per tonne **captured/transported**, while the assignment metric is per tonne **lifecycle CO2e avoided**.

These denominators are not identical.

The model therefore calculates annual costs and divides by lifecycle avoided emissions rather than comparing USD/tCO2 transported directly to S$/tCO2e avoided.

## Falsification trigger

A future central scenario that cannot leave sufficient budget after defensible CCS T&S and capture costs to annualise the nuclear/integration system fails the assignment cost criterion, even if technically feasible.

This is now a primary project falsification test.
