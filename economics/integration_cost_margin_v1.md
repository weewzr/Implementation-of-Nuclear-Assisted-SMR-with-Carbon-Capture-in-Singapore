# Integration-cost margin for shared HTGR architecture v1

## Source-backed IHX/secondary-loop anchor

IAEA TECDOC-1682 reports a preliminary GTHTR300C construction-cost breakdown for:
- 600 MWth reactor;
- 202 MWe electricity;
- 170 MW high-temperature process heat.

The listed cost of:

**IHX + secondary helium loop = USD69.0 million**

is estimated from HTTR construction cost.

This is directly relevant to the project's shared/cogeneration architecture because the published process-heat branch (170 MW) closely matches the current ~145-179 MWth process-service envelope.

It is a legacy preliminary estimate and is not a current Singapore EPC quote.

## Independent cross-check

General Atomics GA-A24265 estimates:
- reactor portion including IHX, circulators and piping: USD371/kWth;
- intermediate helium coolant loop: USD43/kWth;
- intermediate-loop O&M increment: USD0.1/MWth-h.

At 170 MWth, USD43/kWth corresponds to ~USD7.3m for the intermediate loop alone, substantially below the IAEA USD69m combined IHX+secondary-loop estimate.

The difference reflects scope/basis uncertainty and reinforces use of a range rather than treating either as exact.

## High-temperature equipment risk

JAEA explicitly identifies IHX high-temperature creep strength and material quantity as manufacturing-cost concerns and has developed a horizontal IHX concept to reduce material and cost.

Therefore integration cost is not merely conventional shell-and-tube heat-exchanger cost.

## Annualisation

Using the project's 8%/25-y screening CRF:

CRF ~=0.09368.

For the IAEA USD69m component cost with zero additional fixed O&M:

annualised cost
~= USD6.46m/y.

At an illustrative 1.276 SGD/USD:
~= S$8.2m/y.

Any dedicated integration O&M would increase this.

## Compare with surviving midpoint reactor margin

The shared MHR-T thermal-share reactor allocation was previously estimated at order S$50m/y versus ~S$52.2m/y midpoint hydrogen-side headroom.

Thus the apparent residual before integration was only order:

~S$2m/y.

The source-backed IAEA IHX+secondary-loop annualised anchor alone is order:

~S$8m/y.

Therefore the representative midpoint shared-reactor case **does not survive** once this integration component is added, even before:
- reformer modification;
- tail-gas recycle equipment;
- additional circulator O&M;
- licensing/site-specific integration;
- contingency.

## Sensitivity interpretation

This does not falsify every shared-reactor scenario.

Higher NG price, lower CCS T&S, lower reactor allocation, lower integration cost, or valuable cogeneration can enlarge the headroom.

But the central favourable midpoint previously identified is no longer feasible under the IAEA IHX/loop anchor.

## Competing retrofit evidence

Modern eSMR literature shows that existing fired reformers can sometimes be retrofitted while preserving much of the original furnace/convection infrastructure. This supports treating reformer modification as potentially smaller than full greenfield reformer replacement.

However, no source-backed cost has yet been identified for converting a conventional SMR furnace to an external secondary-helium heated reformer.

That cost remains an explicit uncertainty rather than being set to zero.

## Current architecture status

Dedicated large HTGR:
- outside current economic boundary.

Shared HTGR, representative midpoint:
- outside boundary after source-backed IHX/secondary-loop cost is added.

Shared HTGR, favourable sensitivity region:
- not yet falsified.
