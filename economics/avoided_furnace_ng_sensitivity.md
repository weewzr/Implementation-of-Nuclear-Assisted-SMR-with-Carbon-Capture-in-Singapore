# Avoided furnace-natural-gas savings sensitivity

## Source quantity

IEAGHG base case supplementary furnace NG:

Q_makeup = 55.94 MW_LHV.

IEAGHG economic operating hours:

8322 h/y.

Annual purchased supplementary-fuel energy:

E_makeup
= 55.94 * 8322 * 3.6
~= 1.676 million GJ/y.

This is purchased supplementary NG only. PSA tail gas is also conventional furnace fuel but is an internal process stream rather than purchased NG.

## Resource saving

If nuclear integration eliminates a fraction f of purchased supplementary furnace NG:

S_NG
= E_makeup * p_NG * f.

The Singapore industrial NG commodity price has not yet been recovered from a defensible public source, so p_NG remains an explicit SGD/GJ sensitivity.

Town-gas retail tariffs are not used as a substitute because town gas is a different delivered product and tariff structure.

## Compare with current required-savings gap

The Singapore-adjusted Case-1A screening comparator currently requires roughly S$16.8-29.2m/y of net savings to move from S$146-180/t toward S$100/t under the fixed direct-avoidance denominator.

If **100%** of purchased supplementary furnace NG is displaced and if that were the only savings channel, the required gas commodity prices would be approximately:

S$16.8m / 1.676m GJ
~= S$10.0/GJ

and

S$29.2m / 1.676m GJ
~= S$17.4/GJ.

Thus avoided supplementary NG could be economically material at plausible double-digit SGD/GJ gas prices.

But this is before charging:
- HTGR heat;
- IHX/secondary loop;
- reformer modification;
- tail-gas recycle;
- circulator electricity.

Therefore avoided furnace NG by itself does not establish economic feasibility.

## Tail-gas warning

Nuclear heating removes the furnace sink for PSA tail gas. Ahn & Lee's 2026 HTGR-SMR configurations recycle PSA tail gas to the process.

Any economic credit for eliminating supplementary NG must therefore be evaluated together with:
- tail-gas recycle compression/separation;
- changed feed/carbon conversion;
- changed capture flow;
- possible methane/CO recycle effects.

Do not credit tail gas as avoided purchased fuel: it is not purchased fuel in the IEAGHG baseline.

## Carbon tax layer

Singapore's 2026-27 carbon tax is S$45/tCO2e.

Avoided carbon-tax payments may materially improve private project cash flow, but are reported separately from real resource abatement cost.

This distinction prevents a fiscal transfer from being counted as a physical reduction in the cost of producing the abatement.
